# Corrections and completed history

While a work-item is active, defects, missed acceptance details and implementation repairs become tasks inside that same work-item.

After a work-item is completed, never reopen or rewrite its task history. A later defect, requirement change or corrective refactor becomes a new maintenance work-item that depends on the completed outcome when appropriate.

If the correction affects future work, update only prospective dependencies. Completed work remains historical truth.
